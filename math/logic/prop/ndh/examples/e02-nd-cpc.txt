
# Examples of natural deduction proofs in
# classical propositional calculus.

# Ex falso quodlibet
efq. ⊢ ⊥ → A, axiom.

# Law of excluded middle
lem. ⊢ A ∨ ¬A, axiom.

1. 1 ⊢ ¬¬A, hypo.
2. ⊢ A ∨ ¬A, lem.
3. 3 ⊢ A, hypo.
4. 4 ⊢ ¬A, hypo.
5. 1, 4 ⊢ ⊥, neg_elim 1 4.
6. 1, 4 ⊢ A, subj_elim efq 5.
7. 1 ⊢ A, disj_elim 2 3 6.
dne. ⊢ ¬¬A → A, subj_intro 7.

1. 1 ⊢ ¬B → ¬A, hypo.
2. 2 ⊢ A, hypo.
3. 3 ⊢ ¬B, hypo.
4. 1, 3 ⊢ ¬A, subj_elim 1 3.
5. 1, 2, 3 ⊢ ⊥, neg_elim 4 2.
6. 1, 2 ⊢ ¬¬B, neg_intro 5.
7. ⊢ ¬¬B → B, dne.
8. 1, 2 ⊢ B, subj_elim 7 6.
9. 1 ⊢ A → B, subj_intro 8.
contraposition_rev. ⊢ (¬B → ¬A) → A → B, subj_intro 9.
