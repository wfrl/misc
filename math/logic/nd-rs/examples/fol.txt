
# Examples of pure first-order logic

1. 1 ⊢ A ∧ ∀x. P x, hypo.
2. 1 ⊢ A, conj_eliml 1.
3. 1 ⊢ ∀x. P x, conj_elimr 1.
4. 1 ⊢ P u, uq_elim 3.
5. 1 ⊢ A ∧ P u, conj_intro 2 4.
6. 1 ⊢ ∀x. A ∧ P x, uq_intro 5.
7. ⊢ A ∧ (∀x. P x) → (∀x. A ∧ P x), subj_intro 6.

1. 1 ⊢ ¬∃x. P x, hypo.
2. 2 ⊢ P u, hypo.
3. 2 ⊢ ∃x. P x, ex_intro 2.
4. 1, 2 ⊢ ⊥, neg_elim 1 3.
5. 1 ⊢ ¬P u, neg_intro 4.
6. 1 ⊢ ∀x. ¬P x, uq_intro 5.
7. ⊢ (¬∃x. P x) → (∀x. ¬P x), subj_intro 6.

1. 1 ⊢ ∃x. ¬P x a, hypo.
2. 2 ⊢ ¬P u a, hypo.
3. 3 ⊢ ∀y. P y a, hypo.
4. 3 ⊢ P u a, uq_elim 3.
5. 2, 3 ⊢ ⊥, neg_elim 2 4.
6. 2 ⊢ ¬∀y. P y a, neg_intro 5.
7. 1 ⊢ ¬∀y. P y a, ex_elim 1 6.
8. 1 ⊢ ∃z. ¬∀y. P y z, ex_intro 7.
9. ⊢ (∃x. ¬P x a) → (∃z. ¬∀y. P y z), subj_intro 8.
